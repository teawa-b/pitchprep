Real runs on three of the hackathon judges, for a sample live-events startup. Public, professional information only.

How it works

  1. Research the investor

    Web search finds their thesis, stages, portfolio and the public things they host or recommend. Every fact links to its source.

    Web
  2. Score their portfolio for your customers

    Qloo ranks each portfolio company by how much people who use your comparable apps like it, compares the two crowds' ages, and finds what fans of the investor's public interests also love.

    Qloo
  3. Get a one-page brief

    Which companies to name, what to open with, how to fix the deck and what they will ask. Any name Qloo did not return is flagged.
